Adulting Is Hard.

Every day feels like survival.

Work.

Bills.

Pressure.

Deadlines.

Traffic.

Responsibilities.

Somewhere along the line…

we forgot how to play.

"I BE PERSON PIKIN"

was created as a reminder that no matter how grown you are…

You still deserve joy.

For one night, forget the pressure and reconnect with the version of yourself that simply wanted to laugh, dance, play, and feel alive.

ADULTING IS HARD.

COME AND PLAY.

🥤

One Night. No Pressure.

This is your permission slip to let go. Here is what the night holds.

🎵

Live Music & DJ

Dance to the music that raised you. From throwbacks to the freshest hits — the vibe is everything.

🎮

Adult Play Zones

Games, challenges and activities designed to remind you what fun felt like before adulthood took over.

🍹

Drinks & Vibes

Great drinks, great people, zero work talk. Tonight, the only agenda is having the time of your life.

📸

Photo Moments

Themed photo booths and moments designed for your camera roll. You will want to remember this.

🎭

Entertainment

Performances, surprises and moments that will have you talking about this night for a very long time.

🤝

Good Company

Come alone, come with your people. Either way, you leave with stories, memories and maybe new friends.
