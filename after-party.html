✦ THE CELEBRATION CONTINUES

The After Party 🎉

The main event is just the beginning. After the magic wraps up, the celebration continues with exciting post-event activities for children and families.

What Happens After the Magic?

Once the main event wraps up, we keep the energy going with a series of post-event activities designed to send every child home with a smile.

🎨

Creative Wind-Down

Children gather for a relaxed art session — painting, colouring and creating their own WoW Fiesta memory scrapbook to take home.

⏰ 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM
🏆

Awards & Recognition

We celebrate our star performers, most enthusiastic participants and community champions with certificates and prizes.

⏰ 5:00 PM – 5:30 PM
📸

Family Photo Session

Our professional photographers set up special themed photo stations for families to capture their favourite memories from the day.

⏰ 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
🍰

Cake Cutting Ceremony

We celebrate 10 years of WoW Fiesta with a special anniversary cake cutting — a sweet moment for everyone to share together.

⏰ 5:30 PM – 6:00 PM
🎁

Goodie Bag Distribution

Every child receives a special WoW Fiesta goodie bag packed with surprises, treats and educational materials to take home.

⏰ 5:45 PM – 6:00 PM
🎵

Final Jam Session

We close the day with an energetic final performance — dancing, singing and a send-off celebration that the whole family will love.

⏰ 5:30 PM – 6:00 PM
